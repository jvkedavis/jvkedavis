## Hey, I'm Jake 🌊

Originally from Orange County, California, I'm currently pursuing my Master's in Marine Biology at James Cook University in Queensland, Australia.

I graduated from the University of Southern California with a B.S. in Environmental Studies and a minor in Marine Biology.

My academic interests center on **marine conservation, macroalgal ecology, and jellyfish biology**, with a particular interest in kelp forest restoration and applied ecological research. I'm especially interested in combining fieldwork with data analysis to better understand and protect marine ecosystems.

Outside of academia, I enjoy swimming, scuba diving, painting, and spending time along the coast.
